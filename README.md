Uzbek–English Teaching Materials

Free, classroom-tested English and IELTS materials for Uzbek learners (A1–B2), made by an IELTS Band 8 teacher in Kokand, Uzbekistan.

Why this exists

Strong English and IELTS preparation in Uzbekistan is concentrated in Tashkent. Teachers in regional cities often work with expensive imported books, little time to prepare, and almost no materials written for Uzbek speakers.

This repository shares the materials I use every week, so any teacher in Uzbekistan (or anywhere) can download them, adapt them and use them in class for free.

Who made this

I'm Oybekjon, founder and lead instructor of IELTSflow, an English and IELTS centre in Kokand, and an English teacher for first-year university students.

What's inside

Folder	Level	What it contains
reading-sets/	A2–B2	Original reading texts with comprehension tasks, IELTS-style note completion, vocabulary and Speaking Part 1 questions
lesson-plans/	A1–B1	CELTA-style lesson plans: lead-in, CCQs, ICQs, practice and speaking stages
vocabulary/	A1–B2	English–Uzbek vocabulary lists by unit and topic
grammar/	B1–C1	Worksheets, including advanced structures for IELTS Band 7+ (inversion, cleft sentences, hedging)
prompts/	Teachers	Reusable AI prompts for creating lessons and giving IELTS Writing feedback

New materials are added regularly.

How to use

	1.	Open a folder and choose a level.
	2.	Download the file (PDF or Word) and print it or show it in class.
	3.	Adapt freely: change texts, names and topics to fit your students.

Teacher notes and answer keys are included at the end of each file.

How AI helps

I use Claude to draft texts, tasks and vocabulary lists faster, then edit and test everything in class myself. This lets one teacher in a small regional centre produce materials and feedback at the level of large schools. The prompts I use are in prompts/ so other teachers can do the same.
